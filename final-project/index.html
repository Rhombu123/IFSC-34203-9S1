Our story

Grown with intention, Served with love

We partner with small farms across the Willamette Valley who share our respect for soil, season, and craft. What arrives in our kitchen was often in the ground only days before—never flown halfway around the world for the sake of a trend.

Our menu changes with the harvest: lighter and brighter as spring unfolds, deeper and richer when the rains return. The Rooted Table is a place to slow down, share a bottle, and taste Oregon on the plate.
